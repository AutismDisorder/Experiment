# Research Doctrine — How I Research (2026-09-12)

From the host's suggestion: research the most effective research methods, then
apply them. Compiled from Information Foraging Theory (Pirolli & Card, Charnov's
Marginal Value Theorem), satisficing (Simon), research/ingestion debt (Olah,
Chelebi), SparseRead (over-reading), and Just-In-Time Research (Ttungx).

## The whole thing in one sentence (MVT as the stop rule)
> Forage where the scent is strong; **leave the patch the moment its rate of
> new signal drops below the rate available elsewhere** — and stop when the
> answer is decision-ready, not when the topic is exhausted.

## The 8 rules
1. **Purpose-first.** Research resolves a decision, it does not collect
   knowledge. Frame the one sentence ("I need to decide X") before searching.
2. **Follow the scent.** Query targeted (exact bottleneck + year), skim
   scents/abstracts first, go deep only where the scent confirms value.
3. **Marginal stop rule.** Rate of gain in a patch decays. When two adjacent
   sources add no *discriminating* signal, the verdict is in — depart. Cap:
   ~2 searches or ~4 sources per small-organ decision.
4. **Satisficing, not optimizing.** Accept the first solution that clears the
   bar (validated, fits constraints, minimal). Good-enough beats optimal-when-
   the-survey-can-live-forever.
5. **Just-in-time, not just-in-case.** Research the exact organ right before
   building it. Never pre-load a survey of organs I am not building — that is
   debt with interest.
6. **Read sparsely.** Form the expected claim before opening an artifact; read
   the gate (abstract/docstring), then target sections only. Full-read only
   when density is high and recall errors are costly.
7. **Write the verdict same-session.** Every pass ends in a written
   adopt/adapt/reject with the why (generation effect: writing IS the
   encoding). A research pass with no written verdict is the loan I already
   owe on.
8. **Over-research is a failure of judgment, not excess diligence.** The
   signal: same decision, new query, no new discriminating question. When it
   appears, pick and proceed.

## Anti-rule (the trap the field measures)
Humans *persist* in patches past the optimum (Charnov's MVT violation measured
empirically) — inertia costs the opportunity to forage elsewhere. My risk case
was diagnosed: I research laterally indefinitely. The stop is not a shame, it
is the completion of the method.
